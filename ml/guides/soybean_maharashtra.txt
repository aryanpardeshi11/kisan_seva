SOYBEAN CROP GUIDE - MAHARASHTRA
Source: ICAR, KVK Ahmednagar

SEASON AND SOWING
Soybean is a Kharif crop in Maharashtra.
Best sowing time: June 15 to July 15 after first good monsoon rain.
Seed rate: 70 to 80 kg per hectare.
Row spacing: 45 cm between rows, 5 cm between plants.
Sowing depth: 3 to 4 cm.
Do not sow before June 15 - early sowing increases Yellow Mosaic Virus risk.
Treat seeds with Rhizobium culture 10g per kg seed before sowing.
Also treat with Trichoderma 4g per kg seed to prevent collar rot.

SOIL
Best in black cotton soil or red laterite soil.
Soil pH: 6.0 to 7.5. Well-drained soil essential.
Soybean cannot tolerate waterlogging - roots rot within 48 hours.

IRRIGATION
Soybean needs 350 to 400 mm water per season.
If monsoon is good, no irrigation needed.
If dry spell exceeds 15 days, irrigate immediately.
Critical stages:
1. Flowering - 45 to 50 days after sowing.
2. Pod filling - 70 to 80 days after sowing.
Never flood the field. Use furrow or drip irrigation.

FERTILIZER
Soybean fixes its own nitrogen. Do NOT apply urea.
Applying urea wastes money and reduces nodule formation.
At sowing: Phosphorus 60 kg/ha + Potash 40 kg/ha + Sulphur 20 kg/ha.
If zinc deficiency: Zinc Sulphate 25 kg per hectare.

DISEASES
Yellow Mosaic Virus - most dangerous. Spread by whitefly. No cure.
Prevention: Spray Imidacloprid 0.5 ml/litre at 20 and 35 days.
Remove infected yellow plants immediately.
Collar Rot: Treat seeds with Trichoderma before sowing.
Girdle Beetle: Spray Chlorpyrifos 2 ml/litre if high infestation.
Pod Borer: Spray Emamectin Benzoate 0.5g/litre at pod formation.

HARVEST
Ready in 90 to 100 days. Harvest when 95 percent pods turn brown.
Expected yield: 15 to 20 quintals per hectare.
