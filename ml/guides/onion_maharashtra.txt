ONION CROP GUIDE - MAHARASHTRA
Source: ICAR, NHRDF Nashik

SEASON AND SOWING
Three seasons in Maharashtra:
1. Kharif: Nursery June-July, transplant August-September.
2. Late Kharif: Nursery August-September, transplant October-November.
3. Rabi (best quality): Nursery October-November, transplant December-January.
Rabi onion has longest storage life and fetches highest price.
Varieties for Nashik: Bhima Super, Agrifound Light Red.
Seed rate: 8 to 10 kg per hectare. Transplant 6 to 8 week old seedlings.
Spacing: 15 cm between rows, 10 cm between plants.

IRRIGATION
Irrigate every 7 to 10 days in winter, every 5 to 7 days in summer.
Critical stages: transplanting, bulb initiation 60 days, bulb development 75 to 90 days.
Stop irrigation 10 to 15 days before harvest.
Over-irrigation causes purple blotch and neck rot. Avoid waterlogging.

FERTILIZER
At transplanting: DAP 100 kg/ha + Potash 100 kg/ha.
At 30 days: Urea 50 kg per hectare.
At 50 days: Urea 50 kg/ha + Sulphur 20 kg/ha.
Sulphur is very important for onion pungency and storage quality.

DISEASES AND PESTS
Purple Blotch - most common in Maharashtra. Purple spots on leaves.
Spray Mancozeb 2.5g/litre every 7 to 10 days from 30 days after transplanting.
Downy Mildew: Spray Metalaxyl+Mancozeb 2.5g/litre.
Thrips: Most damaging pest. Spray Spinosad 0.5ml/litre in evening.

HARVEST
Ready in 100 to 110 days after transplanting.
Harvest when 50 percent tops fall. Cure for 2 to 3 weeks before storage.
Expected yield: 25 to 35 tonnes per hectare for Rabi onion.
