TOMATO CROP GUIDE - MAHARASHTRA
Source: ICAR, MPKV Rahuri

SEASON
Rabi best quality: Nursery August-September, transplant October-November.
Hybrid varieties: Abhinav, Rupali, Naveen, Avinash-2.
Transplant 25 to 30 day old seedlings. Spacing: 60x45 cm.

IRRIGATION
Needs 600 to 700 mm water per season.
Irrigate every 5 to 7 days in winter, every 3 to 4 days in summer.
Use drip irrigation - prevents leaf diseases.
Never irrigate in evening - promotes late blight.
Irregular irrigation causes blossom end rot and fruit cracking.

FERTILIZER
At transplanting: DAP 150 kg/ha + Potash 100 kg/ha.
At 30 days: Urea 50 kg per hectare.
At flowering: Potash 50 kg/ha extra for fruit color and firmness.
Calcium Nitrate spray 1 percent to prevent blossom end rot.

DISEASES
Early Blight: Dark spots with rings on older leaves. Spray Mancozeb 2.5g/litre.
Late Blight: Water-soaked lesions, spreads very fast in monsoon.
Spray Metalaxyl+Mancozeb 2.5g/litre immediately on first sign.
Never irrigate in evening during monsoon.
Bacterial Spot: Spray Copper Oxychloride 3g/litre.
Leaf Curl Virus: No cure. Control whitefly with Imidacloprid.
Fruit Borer: Use pheromone traps. Spray Spinosad 0.5ml/litre.

HARVEST
First harvest 60 to 70 days after transplanting.
Expected yield: 40 to 60 tonnes per hectare for hybrids.
