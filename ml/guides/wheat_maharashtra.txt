WHEAT CROP GUIDE - MAHARASHTRA
Source: ICAR, MPKV Rahuri

SEASON AND SOWING
Wheat is a Rabi crop. Best sowing: November 1 to November 30.
Late sowing after December 15 reduces yield due to heat stress.
Varieties: NW 1014, HD 2781, Trimbak.
Seed rate: 100 kg per hectare. Row spacing: 22.5 cm. Depth: 5 to 7 cm.

IRRIGATION
Wheat needs 5 to 6 irrigations for good yield.
Critical stages:
1. Crown root initiation - 20 to 25 days. Most critical. Missing causes 30 to 40 percent yield loss.
2. Tillering - 40 to 45 days.
3. Jointing - 60 to 65 days.
4. Flowering - 80 to 85 days.
5. Grain filling - 100 to 105 days.
Each irrigation: apply 5 to 6 cm water depth.

FERTILIZER
At sowing: DAP 120 kg/ha + Potash 60 kg/ha.
At 20 days: Urea 60 kg per hectare.
At 40 days: Urea 30 kg per hectare.
Total nitrogen: 120 kg per hectare for irrigated wheat.
Apply FYM 10 tons per hectare before sowing.

DISEASES
Yellow Rust: Yellow stripes on leaves. Spray Propiconazole 1ml/litre.
Loose Smut: Treat seeds with Carboxin 2g/kg before sowing.
Aphids: Spray Dimethoate 1.5ml/litre if more than 50 aphids per stem.

HARVEST
Ready in 110 to 120 days. Harvest when crop turns golden yellow.
Expected yield: 35 to 50 quintals per hectare irrigated.
