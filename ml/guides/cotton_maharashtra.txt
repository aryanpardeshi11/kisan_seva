COTTON CROP GUIDE - MAHARASHTRA
Source: ICAR, CICR Nagpur

SEASON AND SOWING
Cotton is a Kharif crop. Best sowing: May 15 to June 30.
Bt cotton seed rate: 450 grams per acre.
Row spacing dryland: 90x60 cm. Irrigated: 120x60 cm.
Sowing depth: 3 to 4 cm.

IRRIGATION
Cotton needs 700 to 1200 mm water per season.
Critical stages: Square formation 45 days, Flowering 60-70 days, Boll development 90-110 days.
Irrigate every 10 to 15 days in dry periods.
Stop irrigation 2 weeks before picking.
Drip irrigation saves 40 percent water.

FERTILIZER
Apply FYM 5 tons per acre before sowing.
At sowing: DAP 50 kg/acre + Potash 25 kg/acre.
At 30 days: Urea 25 kg per acre.
At flowering: Urea 15 kg/acre + Potash 15 kg/acre.
At boll formation: Boron spray 1 gram per litre.

DISEASES AND PESTS
Pink Bollworm - most destructive. Use pheromone traps 5 per acre from 60 days.
Spray Emamectin Benzoate 0.5g/litre when trap catch exceeds 8 per week.
Whitefly spreads Leaf Curl Virus. Spray Thiamethoxam 0.2g/litre.
Bacterial Blight: Spray Copper Oxychloride 3g/litre.

HARVEST
Pick when bolls open fully in dry weather. Do 3 to 4 pickings over 2 months.
Expected yield: 8 to 15 quintals per acre.
